Hello, World!

Sonnet: Upon a First Greeting

O World, to thee I first lift up my voice,

A humble greeting from a page new-born;

No grander words could be a truer choice

Than “Hello” spoke upon this coded morn.

Though I be naught but tags and simple style,

A single line upon a server cast,

Yet doth my greeting cross the seas the while,

And reach thine eyes, though leagues be ne’er so vast.

Let coders old recall their first such cry,

When screens did glow with these two words alone;

For every work that reacheth to the sky

Began with “Hello” as its cornerstone.

So long as men can browse, or eyes can see,

So long lives this, and says “Hello” to thee.

— after the manner of William Shakespeare
